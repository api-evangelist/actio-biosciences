SAN DIEGO, January 6, 2026 – Actio Biosciences, a clinical-stage biotechnology company advancing the translation of genetic insights into novel precision medicines, today announced that David Goldstein, Ph.D., co-founder and CEO, will present at the 44th Annual J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference on Tuesday, January 13, 2026, at 2:00 p.m. PT in San Francisco, California.
